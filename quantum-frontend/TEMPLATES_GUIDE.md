# 📋 Circuit Templates Guide

## Quick Start

1. Click the **"📋 Circuit Templates ✨"** button in the bottom-right corner
2. Browse templates by category or view all
3. Click any template card to load it instantly
4. Click **"▶ Run Simulation"** to see it in action!

## Available Templates

### 🔗 Entanglement Category

#### Bell State
- **Qubits**: 2
- **Gates**: H, CNOT
- **What it does**: Creates the state (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2
- **Learn**: Fundamental quantum entanglement - measuring one qubit instantly determines the other!

#### GHZ State (3-Qubit)
- **Qubits**: 3
- **Gates**: H, CNOT (x2)
- **What it does**: Creates (|000⟩ + |111⟩)/√2
- **Learn**: Three-way entanglement - all qubits correlated!

#### Entanglement + Measurement
- **Qubits**: 2
- **Gates**: H, CNOT, M (x2)
- **What it does**: Shows measurement correlation
- **Learn**: "Spooky action at a distance" - both measurements always match!

### 🎯 Algorithm Category

#### Deutsch Algorithm (Simplified)
- **Qubits**: 2
- **Gates**: X, H (x3), M
- **What it does**: Determines if a function is constant or balanced
- **Learn**: First quantum algorithm with advantage over classical - solves in 1 query vs 2!

### ⚛️ Gate Demonstrations

#### Quantum NOT (X Gate)
- **Qubits**: 1
- **Gates**: X
- **What it does**: Flips |0⟩ to |1⟩
- **Learn**: Quantum version of classical NOT gate

#### Superposition (Hadamard)
- **Qubits**: 1
- **Gates**: H
- **What it does**: Creates (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2
- **Learn**: The essence of quantum computing - being in multiple states simultaneously!

#### Phase Flip (Z Gate)
- **Qubits**: 1
- **Gates**: H, Z, H
- **What it does**: Demonstrates quantum phase
- **Learn**: H-Z-H sequence creates effective bit flip via phase

#### Three Pauli Gates
- **Qubits**: 1
- **Gates**: X, Y, Z
- **What it does**: Shows all three Pauli operations
- **Learn**: X, Y, Z are the building blocks - rotations on Bloch sphere

#### State Swap
- **Qubits**: 2
- **Gates**: X, SWAP
- **What it does**: Exchanges quantum states
- **Learn**: Move quantum information between qubits

### 📏 Measurement Category

#### Measurement Collapse
- **Qubits**: 1
- **Gates**: H, M
- **What it does**: Superposition → classical state
- **Learn**: Measurement destroys superposition randomly!

#### Entangled Measurement
- **Qubits**: 2
- **Gates**: H, CNOT, M (x2)
- **What it does**: Measure entangled pair
- **Learn**: Both always measure same value (00 or 11)

## How Templates Work

### Loading a Template
When you load a template:
1. ✅ Circuit is cleared
2. ✅ Correct number of qubits set
3. ✅ Gates placed automatically
4. ✅ Connections (CNOT/SWAP) created
5. ✅ Info panel shows explanation

### After Loading
- **Info Panel**: Purple box shows circuit explanation
- **Dismiss**: Click ✕ to hide explanation
- **Modify**: You can add/remove gates after loading
- **Reset**: Clear everything with Reset button
- **Simulate**: Run to see quantum behavior!

## Learning Path

### Beginner (Start Here!)
1. **Quantum NOT** - Understand basic gate operation
2. **Superposition** - See quantum "both states"
3. **Measurement Collapse** - Observe randomness

### Intermediate
4. **Bell State** - Your first entanglement!
5. **Phase Flip** - Learn about quantum phase
6. **State Swap** - Move quantum information

### Advanced
7. **GHZ State** - Multi-qubit entanglement
8. **Deutsch Algorithm** - Quantum advantage!
9. **Entangled Measurement** - Quantum correlation

## Tips

💡 **Run Multiple Times**: Many templates have randomness (especially measurements) - run them multiple times to see different outcomes!

💡 **Modify Templates**: Templates are starting points - add more gates, change qubits, experiment!

💡 **Read Explanations**: The purple info panel explains what's happening - don't skip it!

💡 **Watch the Visualizer**: Right panel shows qubit states updating in real-time

💡 **Combine Concepts**: Try combining ideas from different templates

## Creating Your Own

After exploring templates:
1. Start with a template
2. Modify it gradually
3. Observe how changes affect results
4. Build understanding step by step
